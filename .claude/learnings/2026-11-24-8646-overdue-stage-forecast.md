# #8646 · Overdue stages in the daily training forecast

The forecast released the entire binding when the first start field proved a non-start. Deadline settlement resolves the later stage independently, so the field could still have no result and no safe training source. Evaluate the scheduled time in the forecast and preserve earlier sources when applying the pending field, as the sweep does. The integration test exercises the normal forecast loader immediately before and after the scheduled instant and compares placement and fatigue to deadline settlement.
