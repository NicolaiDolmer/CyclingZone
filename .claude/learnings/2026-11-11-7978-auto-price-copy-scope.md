# Auto-price copy follows the viewer/pair contract

Refs #2176. Owner explicitly requested the five-language correction on PR #7978.

After a rollout becomes pair-scoped, existing universal visibility wording is
no longer true. Check the actual listing projection and negotiation boundary,
not only the viewer-level Help gate. Keep Help and nearby seller/edit hints
consistent: beta requires access for both parties; unrelated pairs retain
ordinary negotiation. Started public auctions remain open to other bidders.

A reservation reduces available purchasing funds; it does not guarantee that a
winner will remain solvent after every mandatory debit. Describe the protection
without an absolute payment promise, and cross-check finalization refusal paths.

For a rebased release-note branch, preserve the full current main history and
allocate the explicitly requested new version/date. A former merge-conflict
resolution can otherwise land against another entry with the same old version.
Release copy remains held until the owner-approved activation day.
