# #8034 Roster capacity during settlement

The first leg of an atomic swap is an acquisition in isolation. The roster trigger now credits only the opposite locked rider, and the swap validates both resulting destination squads before it commits.

A capacity rejection during transfer settlement must withdraw the confirmed terms through the existing conditional update. Otherwise both confirmations remain set on an offer that can no longer settle or be cancelled.

A deferred swap keeps each rider with the old club for race attribution while reserving their place at the new club. Counting both memberships cannot decide whether a later replacement grows a squad. Compare the outgoing squad before either row changes with the incoming squad after its sale patch, and carry the senior growth result into the paid reservation check.
