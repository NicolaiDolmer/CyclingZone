The migrated realtime hook invoked visual callbacks and waited 400ms before invalidating the viewer scope. Native BEFORE measurement observed price/end at 420–423ms and eight resource reads for every event, including an unrelated bid. The toast could therefore show committed data before the price/end in the row.

The correction gives the existing canonical auction snapshot a testable realtime owner. It publishes whitelisted committed fields and terminal removal before callbacks, supplies the actual pre-patch row, preserves unchanged joined/private fields, and hydrates unknown names in the data layer. Pure public deltas do not refetch. Unknown membership is reconciled on the exact auction key; own bids require private proxy/budget confirmation, and own settlement also requires the exact bootstrap-team read. No amount is inferred as a proxy ceiling or deducted from balance.

Read journals are scoped to in-flight reads and cleared on finish/abort/disposal. Failed or cancelled money reconciliation retains uncertainty across remount.
