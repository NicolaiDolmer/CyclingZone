# Date receipts after race-day ticks

Problem: changing tick identity from date to race-day left a latest-run query,
one history card per tick and a first-date ability snapshot in date-based UI.
Players could see a fraction of development alongside the day's condition.

Fix: preserve ordered activity evidence and project one date/season/rider receipt.
Read the last recorded race-day snapshot for development charts without data repair.
Incomplete/conflicting evidence must propagate to every consumer, including season
totals, moment fallback and rider logs. Mixed cadence cannot prove date completion.

Regression evidence: explicit-date tests reproduce duplicates, missing slots,
quarantine, cross-season date collisions and mixed cadence. Independent review caught
the downstream null-to-empty-map fallback and completed-day quiet-story fallback.
Visual acceptance and production release remain separate from these local proofs.
