# Ability-led rider value with market input: design proposal

Status: proposal for owner review, not approved for build.
Sources of truth: [ECONOMY_RULES.md](../../ECONOMY_RULES.md), [RACE_ENGINE_RULES.md](../../RACE_ENGINE_RULES.md), [PROGRESSION_RULES.md](../../PROGRESSION_RULES.md). Implementation must read progression rules before altering career projection.

## Confirmed owner decisions, 22 September

- Rider type must not determine price. Equal abilities, age and other relevant inputs must produce equal base valuation regardless of natural type, best-role label or frozen valuation type.
- Market evidence participates from the first activation of the new model. Its influence grows over time. A formula-only first release followed by a later market release does not satisfy this request.
- The displayed rating remains best role now and the badge remains natural identity, under the existing #5435 decision. A displayed rating is a summary, not a promise that equal ratings imply identical market values.
- No production mutation, model/visibility flip or merge is authorized by these design decisions.

## Proposed calculation

1. Evaluate the actual ability profile across relevant race demands. Use a standardized opportunity set, so the rider's club, selected races and team strength do not become hidden determinants of intrinsic value. Include contribution as a helper, not just wins or prize earnings.
2. Account for age and expected development without selecting a different growth path merely because a role label changes. The existing best-role experiment changes both price offsets and projected caps, so removing offsets alone is insufficient. Use actual available development inputs; do not silently invent type-based caps. Existing decisions about replacing hidden potential with training-score history remain prerequisites to that separate change.
3. Estimate what managers will pay from qualified independent transactions and bidding. Similarity is based on abilities and age, not an exact type label. Use actual competitive evidence rather than treating mechanically priced bank sales as independent evidence.
4. Use unsuccessful auctions as bounded evidence that an offered price was not accepted; account for exposure and unreasonable asking prices. Losing bids indicate willingness up to that bid, not a completed purchase. Exclude coordinated/self-influenced observations.
5. Combine the ability-based foundation and market estimate from first activation. Report effective market influence and coverage, not only a nominal global percentage. Increase influence through the owner's agreed stages when independent predictive quality and coverage support it. Initial percentage and evidence rules require fresh measurement; do not infer a new value here.

## What must not happen

- Changing only primary_type, valuation_type or best_role changes the computed foundation.
- A small ability gain causes a discontinuity merely because two role ratings exchange rank.
- Growing rider valuations are described as cash creation. Report valuations, cash and actual transfers separately.
- An aggregate valuation total is treated as an approved calibration target without an owner decision.
- A formula predicts its own mechanically generated sale prices and calls that independent market validation.
- Weak market coverage silently reduces the market's actual influence to almost nothing while the UI claims otherwise.

## Verification before build approval and activation

- Synthetic type invariance and local ability perturbation tests, including rounded-rating ties.
- Equal opportunity simulation with coverage by ability profile and age; compare S3/v3 and intended S4/v4 conditions explicitly.
- Time-separated market training/validation, segmented error and evidence coverage, plus manipulation probes.
- Compare candidate starting market weights using the same snapshot. Show effective influence per segment and both upward/downward moves.
- Show per-rider and per-team before/after values, largest losses/gains and separate cash totals in the owner's private report and then the planned admin preview.
- Full backend verification and required preflight; UI layout verification on desktop/mobile belongs to 1c/1d. Existing scorecard floors do not constitute owner approval.
