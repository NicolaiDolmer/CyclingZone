**Mandate:** [owner-authorized issue plan](https://github.com/NicolaiDolmer/CyclingZone/issues/7993#issuecomment-6797641139), 11 November 2026. Later quality-system blocks remain on #7993.
**Architecture:** `@axe-core/playwright` is a development-only dependency. Typed E2E fixtures require real seeded content and a fixed date. A small pure ratchet module compares rule/node counts against reviewed debt; a CLI reduces that debt from a complete scan matrix. Existing frontend-smoke carries the blocking tests.
