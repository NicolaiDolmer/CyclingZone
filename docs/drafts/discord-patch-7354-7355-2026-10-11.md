# Discord #patch-notes: patch 7.354-7.355 (11/10)

Kun EN, rå tekst (ejeren poster selv). Følger formatet fra 7.351-7.353. Sandt pr. 11/10 kl. 11: official_times_v3 er tændt 09:43, Touren og dagens øvrige løb binder til den ved første etape.

```
**Patch 7.354 and 7.355** (11 Oct)

**Races: new race rules, Tour de l'Hexagone included**
Every race that starts from today runs on the new rules, Tour de l'Hexagone from stage 1. Races that already started finish on the rules they started with.
- **Teams chase when they can win the stage**: a team without an order chases when it has a rider who can win the day's finale, a sprinter on flat stages, a puncheur on hilly stages, a climber in the mountains.
- **Chase always adds power**, and **Leave the chase to others never slows the bunch**. Your own order always comes first.
- **A dangerous rider in the breakaway gets a shorter leash**, also from teams that chase.
- **Time trials**: on a hilly time trial, Time trial is the main ability. Climbing only counts for the uphill part.
- **Sprint trains**: with no Sprint captain, a Captain who is a sprinter gets the sprint train on flat stages.
- **Team classification** uses times without bonus seconds.
- **Form counts in races again**. Form peaks don't count in races yet.

**Training**
- **A rider who changes team keeps all his race days**: he could end up with only 4 of 5 in the evening training. The training now follows him to his new team.

**Academy**
- **Signing an academy rider is fast again**: it took up to 13 seconds, and two signings in a row could fail.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes
```
