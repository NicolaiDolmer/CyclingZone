Patch 7.363 to 7.368 (16 to 18 Oct)

Season 5 race rules are on for races that start now
- Time trials spread out more, so a strong time triallist gains real time.
- Finish gaps come from the race, no more fixed steps of a few seconds.
- Bigger breakaways on hills and mountains, and a new one can go after an early catch.
- A dangerous GC rider in the break gets a shorter leash.
- Strong riders can attack alone late in a stage.
- Grand Tours get harder in week 3.
- Sprint trains only by default on flat stages.

Academy 2.0
- Bring in this week's intake with one click on the Academy page.
- Academy Expansion on the new Club page sets how many candidates you get and lets you give the intake a profile.

When your team does not start
- Planning shows Did not start, and your inbox tells you.
- An injury after you picked no longer costs you the start.
- Riders are free for other races straight away.

Also
- Withdraw a counter offer you sent.
- Messages reach you in the language you play in.
- Rider search finds every name.
- Parked teams no longer train.
- Clearer dark mode, text fits on phones, clearer dashboard.
- Weekly value update at 14:00 Danish time, also across winter time.

Beta: the new stage page can replay the stage and shows its key moments.

Full list: cyclingzone.org/patch-notes
