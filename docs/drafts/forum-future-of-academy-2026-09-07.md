**Titel:** `The future of: The academy and youth teams`

```
Hep!

Third one in the series. Do you want to help me shape the future of the academy and the youth side of the game?

Where it is today: two new candidates arrive in your academy every Sunday, you sign or reject them, and when a rider turns 22 you decide if he goes up to the senior team, gets sold, or leaves. About 100 of you have riders in the academy right now, and almost everyone who reaches 22 gets promoted. Nearly nobody sells.

What is missing is the level in between. A young rider develops in your academy and then just appears in your senior squad. He never races his own age group. The plan is U23 and junior teams with their own calendar, but almost everything about HOW is still open, and that is where I need you.

Some questions for inspiration. You dont have to answer all or any of them :)

- Should U23 races pay prize money, or only develop the riders? Money makes them matter, but it also turns youth into one more income calculation.
- How big should a U23 squad be? Small enough that selection is a real choice, or big enough that everybody gets to race?
- Should a U23 rider be allowed to race for the senior team as well? In real cycling the best young guys get pulled up for the big races.
- Junior level: real racing, or purely development and identity?
- How much attention should it cost you per week? Three teams can easily become three times the clicking, and I dont want that.
- Which games have done youth right? Football Manager, Hattrick, PCM, anything else. What made you actually care about a 17 year old in your academy there?
- What would make you care about a rider in YOUR academy today? Watching him win at his own level? A story about him? A scout who turned out to be right about him?

That last one is the one I care most about. A youth system that is only numbers is a spreadsheet. I want it to be the part of the game you get attached to.

Lets have a chat about it - I will share progress in here when there is news on the youth side :)

Thanks in advance!
```
