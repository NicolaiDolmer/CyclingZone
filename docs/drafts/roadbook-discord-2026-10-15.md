```
Hep!

A quick update on where Cycling Zone is right now.

**The race engine gets a proper lift**
This week I went through the race engine and held it up against real racing: what you've written on Discord, the race data since 28 Sept, and real Grand Tours. The biggest things I'm fixing now:
- Time trials end too close. The top 10 should spread out like a real time trial.
- The overall win is too often decided from the breakaway. GC teams should react when a dangerous rider goes up the road.
- On summit finishes, second place is too often exactly 3 seconds behind.
- Breakaways on the high mountains are too small and almost never win.
- Form peaks from your form programs count in races from season 5.
Everything I know is broken is under Known issues on the roadmap: <https://cyclingzone.org/roadmap?tab=issues>

**Season 5 starts Wednesday 28 October**
- Season 4 ends Sunday 25 October.
- Season 5 runs from 28 October to Saturday 28 November, 32 race dates. From season 5 the season ends on a Saturday, as you voted.

**New right now**
- **Stage page 2.0 (beta):** the stage you're racing today opens first, with the profile, the climbs km by km, what the day demands, your line-up and one button to set tactics. After the stage, your day is on top.
- **Set the day for many riders at once (beta):** on the training page, set today's session for all or selected riders in one go.
- **Your first race:** new managers get a card on the dashboard with their first race, when it starts, and one button to set the line-up.

Beta things show up if you've joined the beta program.
```
