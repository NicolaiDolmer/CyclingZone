# Discord-udkast: patch 7.361-7.362 (EN, ejeren poster i #patch-notes)

Opdateret 16/10 til den mergede tekst i `frontend/src/data/patchNotes.js`. Post først de to opslag i `discord-patch-7356-7360-2026-10-14.md` (ikke postet, kanalen læst 16/10: seneste er 7.354-7.355 fra 11/10), derefter dette. Kopiér kodeblokken råt.

```
**Patch 7.361** (15 Oct)
**Season**
- **Season 5 starts Wednesday 28 October**: Season 4 ends the Sunday before. From season 5 the last race day of a season is a Saturday, as you voted in the forum.
**Races**
- **Stage page 2.0 (beta group)**: A race opens on today's stage, with the profile, the climbs km by km, what the day demands, your line-up and one button to set tactics. After the stage, your day is on top.
- **Race standings fit your phone**: The team now sits under each rider, so time and points fit without scrolling sideways.
- **Youth races warn below 6 riders**: The squad filter on My Team also shows each squad's age limit.
**Training**
- **Set the day for many riders at once (beta group)**: One line on the Today tab sets today's session for all riders in the view, or the ones you tick.
**New managers**
- **Your first race**: New teams see their first race at the top of the dashboard, with one button to set the line-up.
**Interface**
- **Grey text is easier to read in the light theme**

**Patch 7.362** (16 Oct)
- **Cleaner icons**: Arrows, ticks and the close cross now match the rest of the game.
- **Help matches how races work**: The Help pages on breakaways, efforts, Ride easy and GC reactions describe the current race rules.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes
```
