# Discord-udsnit v7.295 (23/9) - kun EN, ejeren poster selv i #patch-notes

> Ordret udsnit af frontend/src/data/patchNotes.js (titel + What changed), jf. docs/PATCH_NOTES_RULES.md §3. Postes efter PR #5569 er merget.

```
**Patch 7.295**

**Races**
- **Stages start on time**: The server now checks for stages to run on the clock, so a stage starts at its scheduled time instead of up to five minutes late.

**Interface**
- **Tables on your phone show the team name**: League tables on phones always show the team name next to the Founder mark, and tables now scroll with the page instead of inside a small box, also in landscape.
- **One bar at the bottom at a time**: Only one bar shows at the bottom of the screen at a time: your cookie choice first, then a new version, then the feedback question. An answer you started is kept.

**Notifications**
- **View details opens the race**: View details on a race message in your inbox now opens that race instead of the results overview.
- **Message threads open at the newest message**: A message thread now opens at the newest message, also when you open it from a notification.

**Board**
- **The boardroom keeps its layout**: The boardroom page no longer breaks its layout in a narrow window or on a large screen.

**Dashboard**
- **The survey reminder goes away when the survey closes**: The survey reminder on the dashboard no longer shows after the survey has closed.

**Forum**
- **Quote the opening post**: You can now quote a thread's opening post, not just replies.

**Economy**
- **No new salary for riders who retire**: Riders who retire at the season change no longer cost a salary for the new season.

**Help**
- **How your national core is decided**: Help now explains how your national core is decided and when it counts.
```
