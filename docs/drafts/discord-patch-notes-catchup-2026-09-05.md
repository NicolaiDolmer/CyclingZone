# Discord catch-up: v7.239 to v7.255 (3 to 5 Sep 2026)

Ejeren poster selv. Hver blok herunder er under Discords grænse på 2.000 tegn, så den kan kopieres direkte som ét indlæg. EN-blokkene til `#patch-notes`, DA-blokkene til den danske kanal. Rækkefølgen er valgt så det spillerne mærker mest kommer først. Ingen tal eller tidspunkter i teksten, kun det spillerne mærker (versionsnumre og produktnavne som "CZ Pro v1.1" undtaget).

Allerede annonceret separat (4/9): sponsorernes divisionstillæg og denne sæsons genberegnede ungdomsklassement, begge fra v7.252. De er kun nævnt kort her.

---

## EN 1/4

```
**Catch-up: v7.239 to v7.255 (3 to 5 Sep)**
It's been a busy few days behind the scenes. Here's everything since the last catch-up, sorted by what you'll notice most. Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.

**Racing and rider pages**
- A sold rider stays in your line-up pool until his race is done, greyed out with "Leaves after this race". Before, he vanished while still racing for you.
- Picking a new breakaway hunter on a stage now moves the role instead of adding a second one.
- A mechanical no longer injures your rider. Abandoning with one costs you the race, not the body; only a crash still sidelines him.
- Rolling stages get their own icon, visible on mobile too, and hilly and high-mountain naming is now consistent everywhere.
- Generated stage races can no longer draw two near-identical time trials.
- Injured riders can now be picked for races that start after they heal, and can no longer be edited by accident once crashed out or injured.
- Race page is cleaner: one stage profile, simpler breakaway hunter picks.
- Days in jersey now counts your real jersey days, and the contract panel explains what a new salary demand follows.
- Fixed a crash on Rankings when checking squad strength for an empty division, and the team-compare checkbox is now clearly visible with a live counter.
- Text arrows across the app replaced with real icons.
```

## EN 2/4

```
**Catch-up 2/4: Planning, team and training**
- Riders, Transfers, My Team, team pages and manager profiles all got tidied up: simpler filters, cleaner tabs, better mobile behaviour.
- Calendar day now shows your full division slate instead of a flat cap.
- Assistant "Accept all" now works correctly on a fully planned squad.
- Interval training comes in three packages instead of one.
- Daily Training is clearer that current value is not the ceiling, and weak abilities can grow further before hitting theirs.
- Help explains why training pace can vary.
- You can now dismiss the contract expiry warning on your dashboard for the rest of the season.
```

## EN 3/4

```
**Catch-up 3/4: Money, board, Pro and sponsors**
- Board requests now show you the suggested change before anything is applied, and a bonus goal can no longer complete itself the instant you accept it.
- The board now judges your finances on net position instead of loan count.
- Teams that ended up without a board are back to normal.
- Race results show a full prize money breakdown, and your dashboard and Daily Training now load much faster.
- Pro now shows and charges in euro outside Denmark.
- CZ Pro v1.1: a visible Founder badge, season-by-season ability history, and saved rider filters.
- Auctions got tidied up, plus a way to hide auctions you've been outbid on.
- Already announced separately: the sponsor division top-up and this season's youth classification recalculation.
```

## EN 4/4

```
**Catch-up 4/4: Forum, interface and everything else**
- Forum: mark all threads read in one click, manager and team names are clickable, four new categories plus an archive, and a thread now opens at your first unread reply.
- Table headers now stay locked at the top while you scroll on Rankings, Riders, My Team, Watchlist, Academy and Daily Training.
- Dead clicks fixed: inbox messages without a destination no longer look clickable, several message types got their own icon, and your balance, salary and division on My Team are real links.
- No more tiny, hard-to-read text on the race page and rider profile.
- Notifications, Discord DMs and club DNA now arrive in your own language.
- Season overview's calendar tab now matches Team selection's day numbering.
- A telemetry hiccup can no longer take the whole page down.
- Texts across the app reviewed for language slips and consistent wording.

Thanks for sticking around while all this gets tightened up.
```

## DA 1/4

```
**Opsamling: v7.239 til v7.255 (3. til 5. sep)**
Det har været nogle travle dage bag kulisserne. Her er alt siden sidste opsamling, sorteret efter hvad du mærker mest. Alle detaljer som altid på cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.

**Løb og rytterprofiler**
- En solgt rytter bliver i din holdudtagelses-pulje indtil hans løb er kørt, gråtonet med "Forlader holdet efter dette løb". Før forsvandt han, mens han stadig kørte for dig.
- Vælger du en ny udbrudsjæger på en etape, flyttes rollen nu i stedet for at der kommer en ekstra.
- En mekanisk defekt skader ikke længere din rytter. Udgår han med én, mister han løbet, ikke kroppen; kun et styrt sætter ham stadig på sidelinjen.
- Rolling-etaper får deres eget ikon, også synligt på mobil, og navnene for kuperet og højbjerg er nu ens overalt.
- Genererede etapeløb kan ikke længere trække to næsten ens enkeltstarter.
- Skadede ryttere kan nu udtages til løb der starter efter skaden er ovre, og kan ikke længere redigeres ved en fejl, når de er styrtet ud eller skadede.
- Løbssiden er ryddet op: én etapeprofil, nemmere valg af udbrudsjæger.
- Dage i trøje tæller nu dine rigtige trøjedage, og kontraktpanelet forklarer hvad et nyt lønkrav følger.
- Rettet et nedbrud på Ranglisten ved tjek af holdstyrke for en tom division, og compare-afkrydsningsfeltet for hold er nu tydeligt synligt med en fast tæller.
- Tekstpile i hele appen erstattet af rigtige ikoner.
```

## DA 2/4

```
**Opsamling 2/4: Planlægning, hold og træning**
- Ryttere, Transfers, Mit hold, holdsider og managerprofiler er alle ryddet op: enklere filtre, renere faner, bedre mobil-adfærd.
- Kalenderdagen viser nu hele din divisions program i stedet for et fast loft.

- Du kan nu lukke kontrakt-udløbsbeskeden på dit dashboard for resten af sæsonen.
```
