# Discord-udkast: patch 7.356–7.360 (EN, ejeren poster i #patch-notes)

Discord #patch-notes stod stille på 7.355 (11/10, audit 14/10). To opslag, hver under 2.000 tegn. Kopiér kodeblokkene råt. Erstatter de enkelte udkast for 7.357 og 7.359.

## Opslag 1 (7.356–7.358)

```
**Patch 7.356** (11 Oct)
**Races**
- **Mountain gaps are the same in every division**: On a summit finish, the gap to 10th place is now about the same in every division. Applies to races that start from now on.
- **Time gaps in the youth and team classifications**: Stage race results now show the gap to the leader in these classifications too.

**Patch 7.357** (11 Oct)
**Training**
- **Riders who drop out of a stage race train again the next day**: A rider who drops out, for example on the time limit, follows your training program again from the next day.
**Squads**
- **Moving a rider to another squad clears his old race entries**: When a rider moves squad, he is taken off the upcoming races of his old squad.

**Patch 7.358** (12 Oct)
**Notifications**
- **Unread count stays in sync, and you're told when you're outbid**: Mark everything as read and the menu count clears at once, also in other tabs. You get a notification when someone outbids you, and no email about your own bids.
**Squads**
- **Expiring badge and correct age on U23 and junior squads**: U23 and junior squads show the same Expiring badge as the senior team. Graduation Day shows the rider's age in the season.
```

## Opslag 2 (7.359–7.360)

```
**Patch 7.359** (13 Oct)
**App**
- **Install Cycling Zone as an app**: On Android, open cyclingzone.org in Chrome, tap ⋮ and choose "Install app". Home-screen icon, full screen and shortcuts to Auctions, Today's stages and My team.
**Scouting**
- **Scouting missions search the whole market again**: Missions now search every free agent. If your report was lost to the bug, the 6,000 has been refunded.
**Dashboard**
- **Today's stages loads faster**

**Patch 7.360** (14 Oct)
**Economy**
- **Rider values no longer change at the season switch**: Values only update in the Sunday run, from 14:00 Danish time every Sunday.
**Finance**
- **A rider you bought no longer shows as sold** in your finance history.
**Races**
- **Auto-selection works again for teams with many race entries**: It no longer picks a rider who is already booked.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes
```
