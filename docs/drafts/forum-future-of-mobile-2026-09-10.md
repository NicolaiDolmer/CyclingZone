**The future of: Mobile**

Hep!

Fourth one in the series. Do you want to help me shape the future of the game on your phone?

Where it is today: you can do most things on the phone, but the pages with a lot of numbers are a mess. I have changed the tables back and forth a few times now. First they scrolled sideways and the rider names disappeared. Then I locked the name column. Today I changed it again: a table on the phone shows the name plus three columns, you tap a chip above the table to swap a column in, and "Full table" opens everything with the name locked on the left. That is live now and it stays while we talk.

Honestly I keep changing the same thing because I cant decide what is best. Probably because none of it is best yet. So I would rather ask you.

What you have told me so far, on Discord and in the survey:
- Finding your way around and finding information on the phone works.
- Transfers, training and tactics are close to unplayable on the phone. One of you wrote that you sometimes dont bother opening the game because of it. Fair enough, that one I need to fix.
- Daily training in landscape shows one rider at a time.
- The rider pages are hard to get an overview of.
- The search field on the watchlist is hidden.
- Stage results are hard to find from the phone.

Some questions for inspiration. You dont have to answer all or any of them :)
- What is good on the phone right now? I need to know what to keep.
- What is bad?
- What is really bad, the kind that makes you close the game?
- If I can only fix one page first, which one?
- Which page do you open most on your phone?
- Which games do mobile well? Hattrick, Football Manager, anything else. What do they do that we dont?
- Should the phone show the same numbers as the desktop, just laid out differently, or should it be its own simpler thing?

Screenshots help a lot. Danish or English, whatever is easiest.

Lets have a chat about it - I will share progress in here when there is news on the mobile side :)

Thanks in advance!

Dolmer
