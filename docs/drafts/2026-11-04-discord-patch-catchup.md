# Discord #patch-notes: catch-up 7.387 to 7.399 (udkast 4/11, kun EN)

Discord har ikke fået patch notes siden 7.382–7.386 (27/10). Tre beskeder, hver under 2.000 tegn. Postes i rækkefølge.

## Besked 1/3

```
**Patch notes 7.387 – 7.399** (30 Oct – 4 Nov), 1/3

**Races and race film**
- Dangerous breakaways are kept on a leash: the peloton reels in a break that threatens the overall lead, and controls the gap on flat and hilly stages again.
- Queen stages stay closer: on mountain stages with a short descent to the line, the favourites are no longer spread unrealistically far over the last summit. Climbers keep their advantage.
- A new finale: the uphill sprint.
- Descent attacks follow your orders, and the last 10 km switch in a stage plan happens exactly 10 km from the line.
- Ride easy in a time trial costs time and saves legs. Time trials only show orders that make sense.
- The assistant no longer adds a captain attack on the last climb by itself.
- An attack that gains time is no longer shown under Where your riders lost time, also on stages already raced.
- A rider who abandons after a crash leaves the race film at the crash. Riders who drop out stay on the result as DNF with the reason.
- The race film no longer calls the peloton a breakaway after the catch, and the stage page explains why the breakaway held or was caught.
- Team selections no longer change in a running stage race, and a bar tells you when races are paused.
```

## Besked 2/3

```
2/3

**Training and development**
- Race days develop your riders more: a race day counts as a hard session in the abilities the stage demands, plus a little extra by role.
- Level and potential drive development, not age.
- Five new hard sessions: Long climbs, Lead-out, Short time trials, Long time trials and Cobbled classic.
- Lost main ability progress from the start of season 5 is back.
- A day where your rider races and trains counts as one full training day for fatigue. Hard training around a race can cost more, so plan easier sessions or rest.
- Training receipts show a rider's first progress, and new training types stay visible after you switch tabs.

**Tactics and planning**
- Shared captain and luxury helper can be picked in the season planner and show as their own role.
- One route match number everywhere, straight from the race engine.
- Auto-fill fills whole races first, and no longer puts 1 to 5 riders into a race that cannot start. You get a message the day before if you can't cover every race.
- Tactics drafts survive a partial save. You can rejoin a race after withdrawing.
```

## Besked 3/3

```
3/3

**Calendar and dashboard**
- Race library for everyone: tap a race to see its terrain, how well it suits your team and your status, and browse every edition.
- The calendar shows a race as Ready only when you have enough riders, and how full your selection is, for example Ready · 6/8.
- Next race, Upcoming races and Your first race point to your own races.

**Contracts, academy and market**
- Contract warnings reach every team. They stopped after 1,000 riders before, and now also cover U23, junior and new riders.
- Putting a rider on auction pauses his transfer listing.
- New teams get better starter riders.
- Released riders stay free agents, and injuries explain what happened and when the rider is back.

**Also**
- Help explains the newest race rules and the breakaway markers.
- Many small text fixes: no more -0 CZ$, correct auction hours, no false "no mandate" message.

Full notes: https://cyclingzone.org/patch-notes
```
