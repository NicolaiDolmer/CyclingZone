# Discord catch-up: patch 7.345 til 7.350 (7/10 til 10/10) - udkast, ejeren poster

Sidst postet på #patch-notes: 7.344 (6/10 18:01, læst via Discord MCP 10/10). Mangler: 7.345, 7.346, 7.347, 7.348, 7.349, 7.350. Kun EN (ejer 20/9). To beskeder, fordi Discord har et loft på 2.000 tegn.

## Besked 1

```
**Patch 7.345 to 7.350** (7 to 10 Oct)

**Training: everything from the beta is open to everyone**
- **Train now, programs and groups**: Train now settles today's training right away, without a bonus. Riders who trained can't race today, and the selection, the day board and the season matrix show the lock before you save.
- **Programs**: pick a rider, a group or the whole squad first, then one of 22 standard programs. The ones that fit the rider's type are listed first.
- **Groups**: one plan decision for several riders, with a shared fatigue limit.
- **Season points show all day**, and **the daily report no longer crashes**.

**Races: new race rules from the next race start, Tour de l'Hexagone included**
- Results show the real time gaps, so no more wall of riders at +30:00.
- A team never chases a group with its own rider in it, and the top 10 teams react when a dangerous rider is in the breakaway.
- The breakaway follows the stage: up to 8 riders on flat stages, 12 on hilly stages and 16 in the mountains.
- Time gaps on a climb follow its length and gradient, and a descent to the finish only closes part of a gap.
- Races that already started finish on the rules they started with.
```

## Besked 2

```
**Riders**
- **Every rider has Teamwork and Leadership**: riders without a value now have one, set by the same formula new riders get. Points you already trained are added on top. No rating changed.
- **Rider history no longer shows the same team twice** after you win your own auction.

**Races and Tour de l'Hexagone**
- **Two Tour stages have the right category**: stage 11 is hilly, stage 8 is a mountain stage with a descent finish. Routes are unchanged, so check your picks.
- **Race film and stage story show the same kilometre** for each event.

**Money, board and season**
- **Money in your bids stays locked**: facilities, staff and academy signings can't use money locked in auction bids.
- **Board challenges count riders with star reputation.**
- **Expired youth contracts end at the season switch** on 25 October, together with the senior ones.

**Interface**
- **Open pages keep working when I release an update**, also on mobile.
- **Online means looking at the game**: a background tab shows as last seen after five minutes.
- **Pro: saved filters stay saved**, and **the first unread forum reply is gold again**.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes
```
