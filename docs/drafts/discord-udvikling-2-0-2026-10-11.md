**Rider Development 2.0: what changes, and when**

A lot of you have told me the same things over the last weeks: young riders train too slowly, racing should give more than staying at home, aging feels random, and AI and free riders grow faster than yours. You were right. I have now made every decision on how riders develop, and it comes in two parts.

**Part 1: at the season change on 25/10**
- **Aging:** riders keep their level up to and including age 30. From 31 they slowly lose physical abilities. Mental abilities never drop.
- **Racing slows the decline.** The more race days a veteran has in a season, the less he loses. Hard training days count half. An active veteran will barely move; a rider who never races declines like today.
- **AI and free riders no longer develop.** The big jump they got at the last season change is gone. They still age. When you sign one, he starts developing the day after.
- **Values are not touched at the season change.** They only update on Sunday at 14:00, like every other week.

**Part 2: later in Season 5, switched on together on one Sunday**
- **A new development curve:** young riders grow most from 20 to 25, the peak is 27-28, and the last points cost more than the first.
- **Potential becomes a hidden talent** that decides both how fast a rider grows and how high he can go. It is not fixed at birth: training quality, racing at the right level and how you handle him can move it.
- **A race day gives clearly more than a hard training day** in the abilities the stage asks for. The rider's role decides which abilities.
- **Youth riders from the Sunday intake:** you will not see their potential when you sign them. The training score is your first signal. After 5 training sessions you get the first forecast, and from then you can also sell them.
- **No rider loses anything when this is switched on.** Riders who are already above their new ceiling keep growing, just slower, over two seasons. If you have one of them, you get a message with the names.

Part 1 is small and tested against the real riders before 25/10. Part 2 is bigger, so I am building and simulating it fully before it goes live. There is no date on part 2 yet; I would rather get it right.

Questions or ideas: reply here or in #feedback.
