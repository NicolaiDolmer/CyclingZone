**Patch 7.357** (11 Oct)

**Training**
- **Riders who drop out of a stage race train again the next day**: A rider who drops out of a stage race, for example on the time limit, now follows your training program again from the next day instead of resting until the race ends.

**Squads**
- **Moving a rider to another squad clears his old race entries**: When a rider moves squad, for example from junior to U23 or up to the senior team, he is now taken off the upcoming races of his old squad.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.
