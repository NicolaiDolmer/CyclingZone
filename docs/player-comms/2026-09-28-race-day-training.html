Race day or training day?

From season 4, every race day gives each rider a race or a training session. Never both. The only exception is a rest day in the middle of a stage race. This page shows what each one is worth, so you can make the call rider by rider.

1 · One real day = five race days

A season has 140 race days, and each real date carries 5 of them. Every rider gets one tick per race day: a race or a training session. So racing only uses the slots where your rider actually rides. The rest of that date is training, as you planned it.

Same as a classic: the stage is racing, the rest of the date is training. Only a full date without a stage in the middle of a stage race is a rest day.

That holds when the race trains the abilities you would train anyway. Picking the right race is what makes that true (section 5). The exact slot order in a stage race varies with the calendar.

2 · The stage decides what a race trains

A rider who races counts as having done a normal (medium) session. The abilities it trains come from the stage profile, not from your training plan.

Every other ability still gets a small trickle, exactly like on a training day.

3 · How much a day is worth

How much each trained ability grows. Abilities are counted separately: a hilly stage trains climbing, punch and tempo each at the medium rate. It is not one session split three ways.

Training sessionRace dayNothing / trickle

Specialised sessions push their main ability harder, but skip the others. VO2max Climb trains more climbing and no punch.

4 · Your rider still decides how fast

The stage picks the abilities. The speed comes from the rider, on race days and training days alike. A signature ability grows far faster than a weakness.

Age and potential scale it too. Young riders develop much faster than riders in their late twenties, and juniors get an extra youth boost. All of that applies the same way whether he races or trains.

6 · The honest trade-off

Per race day, training can beat racing a little. Per real day, racing one classic costs very little development, if the race suits the rider. Racing pays in everything else.

7 · When it happens

  • From 20:00Every evening, all 5 race days of that date are settled after the day's last race, never before 20:00 Danish time. The "train now" button does the same thing. There is no bonus for clicking it any more, so every team trains on equal terms.
  • 140 per season5 race days per date, one tick each. Several stages on the same date are separate race days, and each one counts.
  • Max +1A rider can gain at most one point per ability per race day. Any extra progress carries over to the next race day.
  • BetaTraining programs let you plan each race-day slot of the week. Races always override the plan for riders who race that day.

8 · Quick answers

The questions I got most on day one.

Can my rider train on a day he races?
Yes. On the race days of that date where he doesn't ride, he trains what you picked. Only the race days he rides count as racing.
Should I switch his training to match today's stage?
No. The race already trains the stage's abilities. Use the free race days for what the race doesn't give him. A climber in a flat race can still train climbing on the other race days.
He rides 2-3 stages of different types on one date. What gets trained?
Each stage trains its own profile. One flat stage + one hilly stage = one flat session + one hilly session.
What happens during a stage race?
From the first stage to the last, he belongs to the race and can't ride anything else. On a date where he rides a stage, the other race days that date are training. A full date without a stage in the middle of the race is a rest day, like a real Grand Tour rest day.
If I change his training now, when does it count?
Tonight. Nothing happens until the evening run after the last race. Whatever plan is set at that moment is what he trains.
His form is very low. What do I do?
Form only builds while fatigue is moderate. After a stage race he is tired, and hard training keeps him tired, so form keeps sliding. Give him easy or recovery days until fatigue is back in the middle, then form climbs fast.
Does the level of the race matter?
Not right now. A stage trains the same abilities in a small race as in a big one. Only the stage profile counts.
Do bigger teams get more out of this?
No. Every division has the same 5 race days per date, the manual-click bonus is gone, and AI teams use the same rules. A team that races less gets more race days to train exactly what it wants.
