## EN

**Season 3 moves to Friday 28 August**

We were due to start today. We are not starting today.

Team selection had three faults, and running a full season on top of them would not
have been fair:

- A rider could be entered in two races that overlap. A rider in a stage race could
  leave it halfway through and start something else.
- The assistant refilled squads that you had cleared and saved, so planning got
  overwritten.
- You could not save a squad with fewer riders than a full team minus one, which is
  exactly the position many teams are in on a busy day.

**New dates: Friday 28 August to Sunday 27 September. That is 31 race days, up from
the 27 we had planned.** There is racing every single day, in every division.

**All selections have been cleared. Everyone picks their squad again from scratch.**
That is deliberate. The old ones were made against a calendar that no longer exists,
and several of them broke the overlap rule. Your riders, money and contracts are
untouched. Only the race entries are gone.

You do not need to do anything yet. We will post again when selection is open, in good
time before Friday, and you will have the full calendar in front of you when you pick.

Sorry for the short notice. Three days now beats a season full of faults.

## DA

**Sæson 3 rykkes til fredag 28. august**

Vi skulle være startet i dag. Vi starter ikke i dag.

Holdudtagelsen havde tre fejl, og at køre en hel sæson oven på dem ville ikke være
rimeligt:

- En rytter kunne udtages til to løb der overlapper. En rytter i et etapeløb kunne
  forlade det midtvejs og starte i noget andet.
- Assistenten fyldte trupper ud igen som I havde ryddet og gemt, så jeres planlægning
  blev overskrevet.
- I kunne ikke gemme en trup med færre ryttere end fuldt hold minus én, og det er
  præcis den situation mange hold står i på en travl dag.

**Nye datoer: fredag 28. august til søndag 27. september. Det er 31 løbsdage mod de 27
vi havde planlagt.** Der er løb hver eneste dag, i alle divisioner.

**Alle udtagelser er ryddet. Alle udtager deres hold forfra.** Det er med vilje. De
gamle var lavet mod en kalender der ikke findes længere, og flere af dem brød
overlap-reglen. Jeres ryttere, penge og kontrakter er urørte. Det er kun
løbstilmeldingerne der er væk.

I skal ikke gøre noget endnu. Vi melder ud igen når udtagelsen er åben, i god tid inden
fredag, og I har hele kalenderen foran jer når I vælger.

Beklager den korte varsel. Tre dage nu er bedre end en sæson fuld af fejl.
