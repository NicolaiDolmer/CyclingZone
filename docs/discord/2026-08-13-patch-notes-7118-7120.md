## A. Patch notes-indhentning → `#patch-notes`

```
Patch notes (v7.118 to v7.120)

All of this is already live. Full detail is on cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.

Riders (Aug 13): 516 riders had their development ceilings corrected. Every
rider has two types, a main type and a secondary one, and both shape how high
each ability can ever climb. A group of riders created between July 25 and
August 12 never got their secondary type written into their record at birth.
The game showed them a secondary type, but the code that works out ceilings
could not see it, so it calculated as though they had none. Their ceilings
have been rebuilt with both types, the way every other rider has always been
treated. 453 came out with a higher ceiling and 63 with a lower one. 511 are
free agents and 5 sit on player squads. Nobody lost an ability he already
had. A ceiling can never fall below a rider's current ability, so a lower
ceiling means less room left to grow, not a rider who got worse. No rider
changed type and no market value moved.

Scouting (Aug 13): The scout button no longer promises precision it cannot
deliver. The tooltip said that scouting a rider narrows his potential
estimate. That is true for the first steps, but not for the last one. Your
scout's own rating sets a hard floor on how narrow any estimate can get, and
for a team running the default scout that floor is already reached at the
second-to-last level: paying for the final level changes the band by nothing
at all. 149 of the 202 manager teams in the game have not hired a chief scout
and are in exactly that position. Nobody has actually lost money on this yet.
Every one of the 15 jobs bought to the last level so far came from a team
that had hired a scout, so all of them got at least some improvement, though
for a low-rated scout it is small. The tooltips and the scouting help now say
plainly that the last level depends on your scout's rating. The floor itself
is a real design problem, not just a wording one, and it is being re-measured
as part of the rating work.

Help (Aug 13): Two help answers said things that were not true. The scouting
help said potential is always shown as stars and a verdict, never as a
number. It is not: the Scouting tab on every rider profile has shown numbers
per rider type for a while now, a current level and a ceiling band. The help
now says so, and it also says how to read that band. A ceiling band is a
range your rider's ceiling sits somewhere inside. It is not a number he can
reach, and the top edge in particular is not a target: the band is
deliberately drawn wider than the truth so the exact ceiling cannot be read
off the screen. Separately, the rating-scale FAQ claimed the recalibration
was a one-time correction and that a rider's number stays put unless his own
abilities change. That was wrong. The scale is anchored to the rider pool, so
refitting those anchors can move every rider's number without a single
ability changing. The FAQ now says that, and promises a patch note whenever
it happens.

Scouting (Aug 13): The scouting report said "High confidence" about a band 10
points wide. The chip next to your scout's verdict said High, Medium or Low
confidence. It never measured confidence. It measured how completely you had
scouted the rider, so every one of your own riders showed High confidence no
matter who your scout was. Two lines below, the same card said the ceiling
band reflects your current scout's precision. With the default scout that
band is about 10 rating points wide; with a top-rated scout about 6. All of
them were labelled High confidence. The chip now says what it measures: Fully
scouted, Partly scouted or Barely scouted, and hovering it explains that the
band's width depends on your scout's rating. The verdict has also gained a
line saying it is your scout's read, not a fact: it is built on his ceiling
estimate, which is deliberately personal to your team. We measured how often
that matters. Across 300 riders seen by 120 simulated managers, 4.7 percent
got a different verdict depending on who looked. Nothing about scouting,
ceilings or the bands themselves has changed.
```

## B. Valgfri kort version → `#general` eller `#announcements`

Hvis du hellere vil have én kort besked frem for hele blokken:

```
Small update on trust, not features.

We went through every screen that tells you something about a rider's future
and checked whether the claim was actually true. Three were not, and they are
fixed: the scout button promised precision it cannot deliver at the last
level, the help said potential is never shown as a number when the Scouting
tab has shown numbers for months, and the chip on your scout's verdict said
"High confidence" about a band 10 rating points wide.

None of this changes a single rider, ability or ceiling. It changes what we
tell you about them. Details in patch notes v7.119 and v7.120.
```

## Noter til dig, ikke til posten

**Hvorfor B kan være værd at poste.** A er lang og teknisk. Rammen (*vi gik hver skærm igennem og tjekkede om påstanden var sand*) er den del der bygger tillid, og den forsvinder i detaljerne. B siger det på seks linjer.

**Det du bliver spurgt om.** Sandsynligvis: *"så scout-niveau 3 er spild af penge?"* Ærligt svar: for de 149 hold uden chefscout, ja, indtil gulvet er rettet ([#3671](https://github.com/NicolaiDolmer/CyclingZone/issues/3671)). Ingen har tabt penge på det endnu, og teksten advarer nu.

**Det du IKKE skal love.** Sig ikke hvor inde i loft-båndet sandheden ligger. At sige "øverste kant er tegnet over sandheden" skærer op til en tredjedel af intervallet væk, og så er det en lækage frem for transparens ([#1543](https://github.com/NicolaiDolmer/CyclingZone/issues/1543)/[#1162](https://github.com/NicolaiDolmer/CyclingZone/issues/1162)). Formuleringen er *"et sted herinde"*, aldrig *"kan nå op til"*.

**Stadig usandt, og først rettet med #3666:** hjælpen siger at de bedste ryttere ligger tæt på 99. Det er sandt i dag og bliver falsk når den nye skala lander, hvor den bedste rytter bliver ~85. Den skal meldes ud i samme ombæring som skalaskiftet, ikke før, og ikke som en efterfølger.
