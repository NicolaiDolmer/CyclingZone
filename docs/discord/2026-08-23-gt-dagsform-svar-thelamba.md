Both of your points are in.

**No day is filled by one Grand Tour any more.** You were right that it was happening: three days in Division 1 had all five stages from the same race, which meant there was nothing else to do that day. A Grand Tour now runs at most four stages a day.

**The Giro was the bad one.** Eighteen stages spread over eleven days, one or two a day. It is six days now, which is what you asked for. The Tour and the Vuelta were already at five.

While we were in there, we also fixed something you have not complained about yet, so I am getting ahead of you: the monuments were the shortest races in the game. L'Enfer du Nord was 155 km. Shorter than every other one-day race in Division 1, and shorter than the classics Division 4 rides. The five monuments now run 255 to 290 km, and their routes were rebuilt to match, so the cobbled sectors and climbs actually fit the distance.

What did not change: the same races, in the same divisions, on the same days. Only the spread inside the Grand Tours moved, plus the monument routes. If you had already looked at the Season 3 calendar, you will recognise it.

Thanks for measuring it before telling me. It made this a two hour job instead of an argument.
