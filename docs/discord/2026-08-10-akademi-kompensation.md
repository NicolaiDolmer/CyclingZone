**Status:** udkast — ejeren poster selv
**Kontekst:** #3576 (tomt akademi + mail-desync), #3577 (efterspillet), #2699 (de 14),

Baggrund for tonen: fire spillere rapporterede fejlen i Discord 9/8, én ventede ~12 timer

## EN

**Academy: your missing intake is here, plus two extra**

Sunday's academy intake went wrong. The candidates that were generated were far too strong
— one 19-year-old came out stronger than the fifty most expensive riders in the game — so
they were removed. That left most of you with an empty academy and, for some, an email
about a squad that wasn't there. Sorry. That combination was our mistake twice over.

**What you have now:**

- **Everyone gets the two candidates Sunday should have given you** — plus **two extra** as
  an apology for the last few days. If you were one of the three teams who did receive a
  cohort on Sunday, you get the two extra.
- **762 new prospects** across all 192 academies. They are generated with the corrected
  calibration: a 16-year-old now starts genuinely raw and reaches graduation level around
  20, instead of arriving fully formed.
- **Rider types are spread properly again.** Sprinters had almost vanished from academy
  intakes (0.8%) while baroudeurs took over two thirds. Both are back to their intended
  share.

**One more correction.** Fourteen prospects still sitting in academies were generated under
the old calibration and came out too strong — the worst had a best ability of 39, well
above an average senior pro. They've been brought back into the youth range. They keep
their name, age, potential and rider type; only the current ability level changed, and it
now lands where their age says it should. Nobody had paid for any of them.

Affected academies: Les flandriens, Purple Rain, Easy Riders, Silveracers, Krapouchi
Cycling Team, Liams Geder, Timmer Racing, Team Bucovina.

**Also fixed along the way:** academy candidates were quietly being born a year older each
season — a 16-year-old was already showing as 17, and would have been 18 by season 3. And
the "new talent has arrived" email now only goes out once the prospects actually exist.

Thanks for the reports in #bugs and #questions-and-answers. Four of you spotted this within
hours, and the detail in those messages is what made it quick to find.

## DA

**Akademi: jeres manglende kuld er her, plus to ekstra**

Søndagens akademi-optag gik galt. Kandidaterne blev genereret alt for stærke — en 19-årig
kom ud stærkere end spillets halvtreds dyreste ryttere — så de blev fjernet. Det efterlod
de fleste af jer med et tomt akademi, og nogle med en mail om et kuld der ikke var der.
Undskyld. Den kombination var vores fejl to gange.

**Hvad I har nu:**

- **Alle får de to kandidater søndagen skulle have givet** — plus **to ekstra** som
  undskyldning for de sidste dage. Var du et af de tre hold der faktisk fik et kuld i
  søndags, får du de to ekstra.
- **762 nye emner** fordelt på alle 192 akademier. De er lavet med den rettede kalibrering:
  en 16-årig starter nu reelt fra bunden og når graduerings-niveau omkring 20 i stedet for
  at ankomme færdigudviklet.
- **Ryttertyperne er fordelt ordentligt igen.** Sprintere var stort set forsvundet fra
  akademi-kuldene (0,8 %), mens baroudeurer tog over to tredjedele. Begge er tilbage på
  deres tilsigtede andel.

**Én rettelse mere.** Fjorten emner der stadig lå i akademier var lavet under den gamle
kalibrering og kom ud for stærke — den værste havde bedste evne 39, langt over en
gennemsnitlig voksen prof. De er sat tilbage i ungdomsniveauet. De beholder navn, alder,
potentiale og ryttertype; kun det nuværende evneniveau er ændret, og det lander nu hvor
deres alder tilsiger. Ingen havde betalt for nogen af dem.

Berørte akademier: Les flandriens, Purple Rain, Easy Riders, Silveracers, Krapouchi Cycling
Team, Liams Geder, Timmer Racing, Team Bucovina.

**Rettet undervejs:** akademi-kandidater blev stille og roligt født et år ældre for hver
sæson — en 16-årig stod allerede som 17 og ville have været 18 i sæson 3. Og
"nyt talent er ankommet"-mailen går nu først ud når emnerne rent faktisk findes.

Tak for rapporterne i #bugs og #questions-and-answers. Fire af jer fangede det inden for
få timer, og detaljerne i de beskeder er grunden til at det gik hurtigt at finde.
