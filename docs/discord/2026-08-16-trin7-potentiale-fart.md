**Potential = speed (the training update)**

The rework of rider development is live. The most important fact first: no
rider lost a single point. Abilities, ratings and race results are exactly what
they were yesterday. What changed is what the profile SHOWS.

- Ceilings are now set by the rider's role, the same for everyone in that
  role. For most riders the ceiling was RAISED, and riders who looked finished
  can train again.
- Potential now decides how FAST a rider climbs toward the ceiling, not how
  high it sits. Top talent moves about 3x faster than modest talent, and
  reaching the very top takes a full career of good choices.
- The profile shows two numbers side by side: the role ceiling (where he can
  end up) and a projected level (where he realistically lands with the
  training you give him). Better scouting narrows the projection.
- Fair warning: the projection is honest, so for many riders it sits LOWER
  than the old potential number did. That is not a nerf. The old number was a
  theoretical maximum almost nobody would ever reach. The new band is a real
  prediction, and it moves when your training does.
- A one time panel on your dashboard shows each of your riders with ceiling
  before, ceiling now and the new projection, so you can see exactly what
  changed for your team.
- New skill day: Race craft. Positioning, tactics and aggression can finally
  be trained.

This is the model the last three fixes were building toward. The numbers you
see today are the numbers the system is designed around. If something looks
off on one of your riders, post it here and I will look at the actual data.
