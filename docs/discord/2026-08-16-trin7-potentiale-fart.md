**Potential = speed (the training update)**

The rework of rider development is live. The short version:

- Ceilings are now set by the rider's role, the same for everyone in that role.
  Potential decides how fast you get there instead. Top talent moves ~3x
  faster than modest talent.
- Reaching 90 in an ability takes about 12 seasons of dedicated, well-supported
  training from age 16. Only the best of the best, trained by the best, get
  there. Nobody passes 95.
- The profile shows a projection band instead of a theoretical ceiling: where
  the rider realistically ends with your training. Better scouting narrows it.
  It is an estimate, not a guarantee (see the new help entry).
- New skill day: Race craft. Positioning, tactics and aggression can finally
  be trained.
- Nobody loses a single point. Riders above their new ceiling keep everything
  and simply hold there.

This is the model the last three fixes were building toward. The numbers you
see today are the numbers the system is designed around, and they are done
moving.
