## Besked 1: Forum-svar (EN)

Hey everyone. This thread has been running all week, and it deserves a proper answer instead of another quick fix. I have read every post. Three honest points.

**1. Last week was too chaotic, and that is on me.**
Rider types changed, then changed again. Ceilings moved, got rolled back, then moved again. Every single change had a reason, but the sum was that you could not trust what your screen told you from one day to the next. That trust damage is worse than any of the individual bugs. I am changing how balance changes ship: fewer, bigger, properly tested updates with clear notes, instead of live adjustments hour by hour.

**2. The "18% setback risk" has never hit anyone, ever, and it is being removed.**
Before deciding this I went through the complete history: the setback mechanic has fired exactly zero times since launch. Not once, for any rider, on any team. It only ever existed as a warning text, and a warning about a risk that never happens only creates fear. It is being deleted from the game now. The real cost of hard training is fatigue, which drives form and injury risk. That trade off stays, and you can actually see it.

**3. The training update landing this week is built to answer this thread.**
The short version:
- Potential stops working as a hidden wall you crash into. Ceilings become flat per role, and potential instead decides how fast a rider develops. High potential grows fast, low potential grows slow, but no more "fully developed" surprises halfway up a skill.
- Riders who look "done" today get room again. One example from the live data: a 19 year old climber whose climbing was capped at 80 gets his ceiling lifted to 93.
- Every rider profile gets a development forecast: where he is realistically heading, and how fast. You see it before you spend a single training session, not after.
- Scouting feeds into it: better scouting narrows the uncertainty on a rider's potential. That is what scouting is for going forward: information you can act on.

On the bigger worry that only race winners can sign top riders and the gap just grows: developing your own riders is still meant to be the strongest path in this game. The forecast makes that visible instead of something you have to take on faith, and the next steps on the economy side, rider values driven by the actual market, are in progress.

If the update lands and you still cannot see a development path for your team, say so right here in this thread. It has been genuinely useful, even when it was rough reading.
