```
Catch-up: v7.256 to v7.261 (6 to 7 Sep)

Racing
- The race page now opens on an overview with the race state, and Team, Tactics, Stages and Results sit beside it as tabs that follow where you are in the race
- Tactics and Team have more to work with: the open stage's profile sits above the table, Tactics gets a sortable Route match column, you can switch a rider's role straight from Team while the race is on, and the yellow jersey shortcut is back

Planning & team
- New squads are now built against the season you sign up in, so you no longer start with a rider who is already retiring
- An academy graduate you put up for sale moves up to your senior squad if nobody bids and you have room, instead of getting stuck with no way out
- A rider's salary stays put when he moves to the academy

Training
- Sharp and flat training days are truly random again, and the fix carries over to injury, retirement and form swings

Money & other
- Bonus targets you accepted from the board are back on the board page after disappearing when you signed your season plan
- Sponsors have their own page now, under Clubhouse next to Board, with your deal, this season's payments and next season's offers
- A backend or docs-only update no longer breaks a tab you already had open

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.
```
