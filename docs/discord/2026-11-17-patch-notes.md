```
Patch notes, 17 Nov

Training
- Train now bonus is now for everyone: it is out of beta. When you press Train now yourself, before your team's automatic training has run, your riders get a small extra training bonus for the day. Automatic training gives no bonus.

Races
- Spoiler shield for notifications (beta): switch it on in Settings, under Notifications, in the card Results and spoilers. It is off by default. With the shield on, your inbox, the bell and browser notifications only say that a stage is finished, with no winner and no placing.

Planning
- Squad deadline in the Planning heading: when a squad is missing riders, Planning shows it as one line in the page heading with the nearest deadline. The box above the tabs is gone, so the tabs and your races come first.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.
```
