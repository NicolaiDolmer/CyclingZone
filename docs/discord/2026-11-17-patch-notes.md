```
Patch notes, 17 Nov

Training
- Train now bonus is now for everyone: it is out of beta. When you press Train now yourself, before your team's automatic training has run, your riders get a small extra training bonus for the day. Automatic training gives no bonus.

Races
- Spoiler shield for notifications (beta): switch it on in Settings, under Notifications, in the card Results and spoilers. It is off by default. With the shield on, your inbox, the bell and browser notifications only say that a stage is finished, with no winner and no placing.

Planning
- Squad deadline in the Planning heading: when a squad is missing riders, Planning shows it as one line in the page heading with the nearest deadline. The box above the tabs is gone, so the tabs and your races come first.

Races (continued)
- Price words in colour on every order: every race order with a price now shows its price word in the same colour as the stage plan, so Easy, Medium, Hard and Very hard read the same everywhere.

Board
- The board shows the same everywhere: the board page and the dashboard now show the same category numbers. The mid-season message no longer points to a board request that does not exist.

Auctions
- Auction countdown follows an extension: when a late bid extends an auction, the countdown ring and the time now change together with the new price.
- Auctions without the first-bid banner: the banner above the list is gone, so the riders come right under the filters. The tour now starts on the page you are on when you press Show me around.

Interface
- Tidier tables and training on phones: rider tables on phones put the name on its own line, and My team shows its figures at the top. Compare on the league page only appears once you have picked a team. On Training, the Assistant and the rider picker sit in one row.
- Calmer look: one gold button per view, the display font only on the rider's name on the rider page, and line icons instead of emoji in game text.

Full detail as always at cyclingzone.org/patch-notes.
```
