# Discord patch notes 8/11 (copy-paste, EN)

```
Patch notes, 7-8 November

New on beta
- Rider card and right-click menu: a small icon after every rider name opens his card with abilities, value, contract and how he is doing in the race. Right-click a name for quick actions like compare, watchlist or a bid. On the phone, tap the icon.

Improved
- The roadmap shows season numbers: no more Coming up or Later. Every item sits under this week, next week, a season number (Season 5 means it comes before season 6 starts) or a quarter like Q1 2027. Several items moved based on your votes.
- Bigger buttons on your phone: small buttons like the language picker and the tabs are now easy to hit with a finger.
- One Show picker in the calendar: My team, All divisions or a single division, with Calendar or Table, month and season on the same line.
- Time trials have their own terrain weighting on flat and rolling roads, from the next time trial.

Fixed
- A team created late in the evening no longer loses its first training day.
- A sold rider waiting to move can no longer be released or sold again.
- Auctions no longer end on the closing minute. They end when the auction hours open again.
- A race without enough riders now says Missing, also while your team rides a Grand Tour.
- Help corrected: rider values, worn-out helpers, uphill sprints and wall finishes, and form peaks.

Full notes on the Patch Notes page.
```
