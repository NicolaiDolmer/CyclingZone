Training program: rotation with pauses, and locked passes

The same week, two ways to keep a plan alive when races and rest land on random race-day slots. Everything on this page is live: tick the boxes, lock a pass, and the weeks recompute. Data is a fixed anonymised fixture (Rider A and B, Race X); pass names come from the Sprinter program.

Sprinter rotation · 10 passes

One list, in fixed order. Passes run in a ring. A race, a rest day or the fatigue limit never skips a pass: the rotation waits and carries on in the next free slot.

    Each rider has their own place in the ring (see "Next" above each week). Riders on the same program can ride different races and still lose no pass.
